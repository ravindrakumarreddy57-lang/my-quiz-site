### Reading Comprehension Set 1 (Q.141–Q.145): Standard Costing & Variance Analysis
**📈 Strategy Note:** *All five questions are based on a single data set — solve Q.141 first (Material Price Variance) to establish the actual price, then proceed in sequence. Know the formulae: Price Variance = (SP – AP) × AQ; Quantity Variance = (SQ – AQ) × SP; Labour Efficiency = (SH – AH) × SR; Overhead Spending = Budgeted OH – Actual OH; Volume Variance = Fixed OH Rate × (Normal Output – Actual Output).*
